# **App Name**: Siklab Academy

## Core Features:

- User Authentication & Profiles: Secure registration and login for both students and instructors, with individual user profiles to manage roles and settings.
- Course & Lesson Management: Instructors can create new courses, add lessons (text, video, files), and organize learning content.
- Lesson Content Viewer: Students can view various types of lesson content (text, video, PDF) directly within the platform.
- AI Quiz Question Generator Tool: An AI-powered tool for instructors to automatically generate quiz questions based on lesson content or specified topics.
- Quiz Submission & Scoring: Students can take quizzes and submit assignments, with instructors able to review and score them.
- Student Progress Tracking: Students can view their progress through courses and completed lessons, while instructors can monitor student performance.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A calm, focused blue (#2E74B8). This hue, chosen for its association with intelligence and reliability, grounds the user experience in a sense of educational purpose. In HSL, this translates to a hue of 210 degrees, with 60% saturation and 45% lightness, making it prominent yet not overly vibrant on a light background.
- Background color: An extremely light, desaturated blue (#ECF3F9). Derived from the primary hue, its high lightness (95%) and low saturation (20%) create a clean, airy canvas that promotes readability and reduces eye strain, essential for focused learning.
- Accent color: A vibrant cyan (#55CCCC). Analogous to the primary color (approximately 30 degrees 'left' on the color wheel), this color provides a lively contrast, highlighting interactive elements, calls to action, or key information with its higher saturation (70%) and moderate lightness (60%), ensuring visual interest without being distracting.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' (sans-serif). A modern and neutral sans-serif typeface, 'Inter' offers excellent readability across various screen sizes, making it ideal for presenting educational content clearly and objectively for both headlines and longer text passages.
- Utilize simple, conceptual line icons related to education and progress, such as books, checkmarks, progress bars, and user silhouettes, maintaining a consistent minimalist style.
- Employ a clean, structured layout with ample whitespace to enhance readability and organization of educational content, fostering an intuitive learning environment.
- Implement subtle and smooth animations for page transitions and interactive elements, such as expanding content sections or quiz responses, providing gentle visual feedback without causing distraction.
